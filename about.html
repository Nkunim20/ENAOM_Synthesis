WHY WE'RE HERE

Every student has a story.

Campus Unfiltered is a concept for an anonymous campus community. It gives students a place to share funny moments, talk about academic stress, celebrate small wins and discuss campus life without making their identity the centre of the conversation.

This student project currently runs in your browser. It uses sample posts and browser storage for the demo, so it is not yet a live campus-wide service.

THE HOUSE RULES

Unfiltered, not unkind.

Help keep this corner of campus safe enough for people to speak honestly.

  1. 01
    No personal details

    Don't publish phone numbers, addresses, student IDs or identifying information.

  2. 02
    No bullying, threats or hate

    Disagree with a take if you want. Don't target or intimidate a person.

  3. 03
    Think before you post

    Don't spread rumours as facts or share claims that could seriously harm someone.

  4. 04
    Keep it constructive

    Funny, awkward, thoughtful and critical posts are welcome when they're respectful.
